90-day delivery roadmap

01
DAYS 01–30

Discover and define

Interview stakeholders, prioritise decisions, confirm data rights and set baseline metrics.

02
DAYS 31–60

Pilot and validate

Build a narrow prototype, define source hierarchy, test data accuracy and usability.

03
DAYS 61–90

Measure and hand over

Measure time saved and data quality; document support, access and change controls.

Success measures: Reduced manual reconciliation time; fewer unresolved data conflicts; improved reporting turnaround and stakeholder adoption. No unverified savings estimates.
