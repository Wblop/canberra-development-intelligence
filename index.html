PARCEL-LEVEL DUE DILIGENCE

REQUIRED BEFORE ANY ACQUISITION DECISION
01 / Zoning & permissions

Confirm lot-level zone, land use tables, height controls, heritage and applicable planning instruments.

02 / Demand & competition

Check ABS SA2 trends, household profiles, comparable sales, competing supply and absorption.

03 / Daily amenities

Assess walking and transit access to schools, jobs, health, retail, parks and open spaces.

04 / Infrastructure

Confirm actual water, sewer, power, roads, digital connectivity, contributions and delivery timing.

05 / Site constraints

Investigate flood, bushfire, contamination, biodiversity, Aboriginal cultural heritage and geotechnical conditions.

06 / Commercial viability

Model acquisition costs, yield, GST/stamp duty, construction, finance, holding cost, staging and sensitivity.

Research method: The 30 areas form a selective national research watchlist, including 20 ACT locations and five SLA-marketed site offerings published at the review date. Context is sourced from public planning pages and the ABS 2024–25 regional population release, reviewed 11 November 2026. Local zoning and land titles have not been verified. ABS source.
